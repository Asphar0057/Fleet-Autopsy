# Demo script (3 minutes)

1. 0:00 Landing. Read the lede, click "Enter the simulator".
2. 0:10 Click "Start with the decoy scenario" (beside the scenario select). Playback starts on Pocket DC, seed 3.
3. 0:15 Set speed 4x. Around tick 350 R6 stops (the decoy), then moves again. Around tick 550 R3 freezes on station 2. Watch the queue bars fill and the badge turn to "Jam detected".
4. 1:00 In the Telemetry card, click "Find the cause". The precomputed autopsy opens under the canvas.
5. 1:05 Step 1, The alarm: point at the accent onset line on the scrubber and the sparkline. Next.
6. 1:20 Step 2, Suspects: candidate faults with score bars, leading hypothesis first. Next.
7. 1:40 Step 3, Counterfactual replay: press Play; observed run and the replay with only the leading hypothesis share the playhead; ringed robots differ. Next.
8. 2:00 Step 4, Root cause: culprit marked in accent on the main canvas, evidence and confidence. Show the table: "Blame the first alarm" picks the decoy and is marked wrong. Next.
9. 2:20 Step 5, Verified fix: before and after throughput curves, recovered fraction, the fix sentence, "verified in simulation". Click "Replay with fix applied" and let the main canvas run without the jam.
10. 2:40 Click "Report", show the template report, click "Copy".
11. 2:50 Close on the Assumptions and limits card: simulation only.
